Pizza

Description

Pizza is a universally loved, savory Italian dish that originated in Naples. It consists of a flattened round dough base topped with tomato sauce, gooey mozzarella cheese, and an endless variety of ingredients like vegetables, meats, and herbs, baked until it achieves a perfect, crispy crust.
